The progression

Where climbers start with us

Almost no one starts on an eight-thousander. The climbers we take highest have usually moved up through the bands with us — a trekking peak to learn the systems, a 6,000 m peak for a first fixed camp, a 7,000 m peak to test altitude and commitment, then a Himalayan giant.

  1. 01Trekking peak

    5,500–6,500 m. Rope work, crampons and glacier travel on a climb attached to a trek.

  2. 026,000 m peak

    A first expedition with fixed camps, load carries and a summit day at real altitude.

  3. 037,000 m peak

    The proving ground — multi-week expeditions, serious weather, the altitude that decides readiness.

  4. 048,000 m peak

    The giants. Supplementary oxygen, Sherpa climbing crew, and a summit in the death zone.
